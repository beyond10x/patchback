# Progress and listening demos on the documentation site

## Outcome

The public documentation site at `https://beyond10x.github.io/patchback/` shows what has been
built and lets a reader hear it:

1. **Progress.** A generated page lists every milestone of DESIGN.md § 13 with its stories from
   the planning store, each story's status and, for an implemented story, the release that
   delivered it. The page states the counts per milestone and the latest release with its
   date. It is regenerated by `task docs-generate` and drift-checked by `task check`.
2. **Listen.** A generated page presents a fixed set of demo voices, each as four things a
   reader can play in the browser: the engine's render of the voice (the "recording"), the
   reconstruction of the default-start fit, the reconstruction of the regressor-seeded fit, and
   the regressor's prediction, clamped to the parameter range, rendered; beside them the voice's
   parameters, the recovered parameters and the loss numbers (start → best, evaluations) of
   both fits. The numbers and the recovered patches are produced by a Rust generator in the
   repository from committed demo inputs (patch files, note files, a weights artifact with its
   normalization statistics and their provenance); the audio is rendered from those recovered
   patches when the site is built and is not committed.

The operator asked for this on 2026-10-25: "make sure we have our impl progress reflected in
the public docs, also provide some demos there, eg synthesizing/reproduction samples".

## Numeric obligations

- The demo set is 4 voices (3 when the measured `demos fit` total passes 4 minutes, Da12-22),
  one note each, under 2 s of audio per file, 16-bit PCM WAV at 48 kHz, mono.
- The committed demo data is text and under 64 KiB; the committed weights artifact is the
  `weights/1` format (10,438 bytes) with its statistics text and a provenance file naming the
  dataset seed, split sizes, training steps, ModelSpec version and commit, and the artifact's
  checksum.
- Regenerating the demo numbers (the fits) runs in release mode and under 5 minutes locally,
  with no fit stopped by its wall-time bound.
- The drift check and the build-time rendering add under 30 s of run time, compilation apart, to
  `task check` and to the site build (Da12-23); the implementor measures both.
