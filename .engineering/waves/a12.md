## Decisions

Da12-0 to Da12-12 in `approval-record:wave-a12`. The `benchmark` verb gains `--kind
engine-baseline|ml-init` (default `engine-baseline`) and the `--weights`/`--normalization` pair
(Da12-2); the inputs are read once before the holdout and `weight_artifact_id` is
`sha256:<hex>` (Da12-3); four arms per clip, the raw prediction rendered once and the seeded fit
with the clip's `fit_seed` (Da12-4); kind-dependent records at format version 1, no verdict
(Da12-5, Da12-9); five listening files on `ml-init` (Da12-6); the library half is
`Benchmark::record_ml_init_result` (Da12-8). For the owner: the Milestone B exit reading
(Da12-9), the listening-file count (Da12-6), the clamp record (Da12-5).
