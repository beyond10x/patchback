# Milestone A: sound engine without ML

## Outcome

A reference WAV generated by Patchback's own synth, plus its known note events, goes in; a
recovered `.patch`, a reconstructed WAV and a report come out, and the recovered patch has a lower
audio loss than the default patch. No neural network is involved (DESIGN.md § 13 Milestone A, § 15).

## In scope

- The Cargo workspace scaffold: root `Cargo.toml`, the crate layout of § 4.1, the CLI entry point
  and the capability list (story:workspace-scaffold).
- std-only WAV input and output, 48 kHz mono (§ 6.1).
- Provenance for external audio at import (§ 12.3).
- The fixed `subtractive-mono/1` voice: oscillator, drive, filter, ADSR, gain, with its eight
  parameter contracts (§ 5.2, § 5.3).
- Patch graph validation and the `.patch` and `.events` file formats (§ 5.1, § 11).
- FFT/STFT, envelope and spectral analysis, the comparison loss (§ 6.2, § 9.1).
- Bounded derivative-free search and its run artifacts (§ 9.2, § 11).
- The dependency gate (§ 3, § 12.2).
- The pinned ModelSpec binary checks the § 8.1 model, in parallel with the rest of A (§ 15).

## Out of scope

ML, phrases, topology choice, feedback (Milestones B–D).

## Exit

Reliable executable patches, defensible audio comparison, deterministic test fixtures (§ 13).

Milestone A exits when story:engine-baseline records a median reduction > 0 over the default patch.
Met/NotMet at 20 % (DESIGN.md § 12.2) is the go/no-go for Milestone B.
