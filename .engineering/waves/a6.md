# Wave a6

Milestone B wave 6, the first under `initiative:milestone-b`. Serves `epic:sound-engine` (unit
1) and `epic:modelspec-regressor` (unit 2): the bounded search `patchback fit` runs, and the
seeded dataset `patchback generate-dataset` writes. A third unit remodels the `Benchmark`
holdout in the specification so `story:engine-baseline` becomes ready.

  a4). Not ready: `story:fit-run-artifacts` and `story:dense-data-files` (depend on units 1 and 2,
