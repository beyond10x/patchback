## Decisions

- Open questions the spec already models follow the model; the `UNMAPPED:` markers stay.
- A bound DESIGN.md gives no value for is a named constant with a documented provisional value,
  listed below when the units report them, and stays open in DESIGN.md § 17.
- Render (unit 1): a sequence's recorded `patch_id` is not compared with the rendered patch
  (`spec/domains/audio.yaml` `RenderPatch`); a failed render leaves no `Render` and no WAV; only a
  non-positive block size is refused; the output WAV is float32; the limiter threshold and the
  render tail after the last note are provisional constants.
- Provenance (unit 2): a library-level import request carrying origin, provenance, licence and
  render id; required on every `ExternalRecording` import, as modelled. `patchback analyze` and
  `compare` are unchanged: DESIGN.md § 11 names no flag for origin, provenance or licence, and a
  flag is a DESIGN.md change (OQ-20 stays open).
- Feature contract (unit 3): the 24 band energies are window-major (attack bands 0–7, body, release);
  the windows come from a note's start and end handed to the extractor, with provisional
  constants where DESIGN.md § 6.3 gives none; the band edges are `BAND_EDGES_HZ` (eight bands);
  `CheckFeatureVector` and `AmendFeatureContract` follow the model though § 6.3 does not name
  them (carried forward as a DESIGN.md note).
